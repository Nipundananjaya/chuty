## 📷 How to Add Your Real Photos

You have **two super easy ways**:

### Method 1: In the Browser (Instant)
1. Open [index.html](file:///c:/Users/Danan/OneDrive/Desktop/bab/index.html) in your browser.
2. Click the **"📷 Change Photos"** button in the top navigation bar.
3. Select your couple photos directly from your phone or PC.
4. Click **"Save Photos ✨"** — done!

### Method 2: Replace Files in the `images` Folder
Simply replace the placeholder pictures in the `images/` folder with your own photos using the same names:
- `images/dating.jpg` (Photo from when you started dating)
- `images/first_meet.jpg` (Photo from when you first met on 2026.01.13)
- `images/gallery1.jpg` to `gallery6.jpg` (Your favorite couple pictures)
- `images/hero_romantic.jpg` (Hero backdrop photo)
